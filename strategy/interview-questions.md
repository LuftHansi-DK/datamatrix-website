# Interview questions for Hans

These are the questions whose answers would change the strategy most. Short answers are fine; numbers can be
rough, and you can answer them in private rather than in this public repo.

1. **Concentration.** Roughly what share of DataMatrix's revenue comes from the anchor customer, and how much of
   that is hours rather than fixed agreements? If it halved within 12 months, how long could you run unchanged?

2. **The anchor relationship.** What have the anchor customer and the incoming IT head actually said about your
   future role, budget or bringing IT in-house? Who there would sign a governance retainer, and who would
   argue against it?

3. **Capacity.** Of the work you did in the last three months, about how much could your agents have done end to
   end today with only your approval? What would you do with the time freed?

4. **What you enjoy.** Which work gives you energy (architecture, security, firefighting, teaching, selling,
   building agents) and which drains you? In two years, how many hours a week do you want to work?

5. **Risk appetite.** Are you willing to be accountable for agents that act in customers' tenants, beyond drafts?
   What does your professional liability insurance cover today, and has the insurer been told about agent work?

6. **Small customers' reality.** What shadow-AI have you actually seen: unapproved app consents, AI note-takers,
   apps or staff sending mail in the company's name, data pasted into public tools? How many customers have asked
   you about AI unprompted?

7. **Pricing today.** Which customers are on fixed agreements and which pay by the hour? How would your best small
   customers react to a monthly AI-tilsyn line on their invoice?

8. **Selling.** Will you do outbound work (talks at local business networks, LinkedIn posts, ERFA groups), or should
   the strategy assume referrals and the website only?

9. **Succession and partners.** Would you bring in a second senior or a partner, or sell or merge DataMatrix within
   five years? Which technicians in your network could deliver an AI-tjek using your method?

10. **Platform stance.** At customers, should agents run on your own stack (Grok Bot and the other harnesses) or
    on Microsoft-native tools (Copilot Studio, Entra Agent ID), which they can keep without you? Where do you draw
    the line on lock-in, both theirs and yours?

11. **Being the face.** Are you comfortable with the website and your public profile being explicitly about you
    personally ("jeg"), including saying publicly that you run your own business with AI agents?

## The three that matter most

- **Q1 Concentration** decides how urgent and how defensive the plan must be.
- **Q2 The anchor relationship** decides whether the retainer move is a renewal or a rescue.
- **Q5 Risk appetite** decides whether "managed agents" is a product or something you advise on but don't operate.
